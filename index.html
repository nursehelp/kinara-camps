How booking works

An enquiry is an application, not a booking. Spots are limited, and we personally choose which groups we can host — you only pay once your group is accepted.

Step 1

Apply with your group details

Fill in the booking form for your night — group owner's name and WhatsApp, plus every guest's name, age and gender. We need the full list to plan tents and seating, so have it ready before you start.

Step 2

Selected groups get an invite

We review every group personally and accept only the ones we can host well. If yours is selected, you'll get an email invite with your booking reference, the total, and UPI payment details — pay 50% and send us the screenshot to lock your spot. If we can't offer your group a spot, we'll email you to say so. Please don't pay anything until your invite arrives.

Step 3

Balance 7 days before

The remaining half is due a week before your camp — 19 Dec for Christmas, 24 Dec for New Year's Eve. Then just show up — ride down with the group if you've added transport, or make your own way to the camp.

Our promise

There is no minimum number. However many guests book — two or fifty — the camp runs, and your night happens. And if we ever have to cancel from our side, every rupee you've paid comes back within 5 working days. No fees held back, no vouchers instead of money.

Good to know

Does sending an enquiry guarantee a spot?

No. Spots are limited and we personally choose the groups we can host. Sending the form is an application: if your group is selected, you'll get an invite email with your booking reference and UPI payment details. If we can't offer your group a spot, we'll email you to let you know. Please don't pay anyone or make travel plans before your invite arrives.

What details do you need for each guest?

Every guest's full name, age and gender, plus the group owner's WhatsApp number and email. We use the list to work out the male/female mix, plan tents and bus seats, and check everyone in on the day — so please send the final list, and tell us if anyone changes.

Where exactly is the camp?

On the Alibaug coast (Revdanda–Mandwa side), a short ride from the jetty. The exact beach and Mumbai pickup points are confirmed with your booking, once our campsite partner for each date is locked in.

Is transport included?

No — the camp price covers your tent stay, food and the full programme, not transport. You can add our Mumbai–Mumbai bus for +₹999 per person on the enquiry form: we pick the group up in Mumbai in the afternoon and drop everyone back the next day after checkout, with pickup points shared with confirmed guests. Or make your own way to the camp — the exact location comes with your booking.

What should I bring?

Warm layers for later in the night (December on the coast gets cool), comfortable footwear for sand, toiletries, a phone charger or power bank, and any personal medication. Tents, bedding, food and the bonfire are all provided.

Can I get a private tent for two?

Yes — add a private couple tent for ₹499 per tent, instead of a shared tent. Pick "Private couple tent" in the enquiry form and we'll work out the number of tents from your group size.

What if I need to cancel?

Full refund (minus payment charges) up to 7 days before your camp — that's 19 Dec for Christmas and 24 Dec for New Year's Eve. 50% refund after that, and no refund in the final 72 hours. Your spot is transferable to a friend at any time, free.

What if the camp itself is cancelled?

The camp runs regardless of numbers — there is no minimum. In the unlikely event we ever have to cancel from our side, everyone is refunded in full within 5 working days. That's the deal, in writing, on every booking.

Who is this for?

Groups of friends, couples and colleagues from Mumbai and Pune who want a festive night out without planning it. You're hosted the whole evening — nobody has to be the organiser.
